# Knowledge OS Starter Demo Source

## Normalized Content

A durable knowledge system separates evidence, reusable knowledge, research history, memory, and decisions.

Raw sources preserve evidence. Wiki notes preserve reusable understanding. Research notes preserve how conclusions were reached. Memory stores contextual history. Decision notes preserve chosen actions and rationale.

Future agents should search compiled knowledge before reopening broad Raw sources. For complex questions, agents should assemble a structured context pack before reasoning.
