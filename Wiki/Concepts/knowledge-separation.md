# Knowledge Separation

## Definition

A durable Knowledge OS separates Raw evidence, current Wiki understanding, Research history, contextual Memory, and Decisions. [C-001]

## Why It Matters

Separation prevents contextual recollection from being mistaken for sourced fact and preserves why conclusions or choices changed. See [[Wiki/Topics/knowledge-operating-systems]] and [[Wiki/Synthesis/knowledge-os-architecture]].
